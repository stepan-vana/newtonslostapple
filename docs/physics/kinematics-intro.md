# Reference Frame, Point Mass

A **point mass** is a body defined only by its position and mass; its dimensions are neglected to simplify calculations.

A **reference frame** is a system used to describe the motion of bodies relative to one another. For example: a roadside post and a moving car. The measured velocity of a body depends on the chosen reference frame, e.g. a moving train and a person inside the train vs. a person outside the train. From the perspective of a person inside the train, the train is at rest relative to them.

# Description of Motion

## Trajectory

A trajectory is the path described by a body or point mass during its motion, i.e. the curve along which the body moves. Example: the trace left by a pencil tip represents its trajectory.

### Classification of trajectories:

- **Rectilinear motion** – motion along a straight line; the trajectory is a straight line segment. Example: a line drawn with a ruler.
- **Curvilinear motion** – motion along a curved path. Example: a circle drawn with a compass.

## Distance

**Distance** is a scalar physical quantity denoted by $s$, with the SI unit meter (m).  
It represents the length of the trajectory traveled by a body over a given time.
