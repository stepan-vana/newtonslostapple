# Kinetic energy
Every object to move requires some energy and also every moving object has some energy (becase of the speed).  
**Kinetic energy**:
$$
E=\frac12mv^2\newline
[E]=J(joule)
$$
You can see that weight is also included here, I can imagine that as a tennis ball and a car hitting a wall at the same speed. The tennis ball will just bounce off, but the car is gonna get smashed
