# Momentum

Is a physical vector quantity, that describes "_how hard is it to stop the body while it's moving_". The symbol is $\vec{p}$ and the definition is:

$$
\boxed{\vec{p}=m\vec{v}}\newline
[\vec{p}]=\mathrm{kg\cdot m\cdot s^{-1}}
$$

## Law of conservation of momentum

In a ideal (closed) system the total momentum **stayes the same**. You can define it as:

$$
\boxed{\vec{p_1}+\vec{p_2}+...+\vec{p_n}=const.}
$$

or

$$
\boxed{\frac{d\vec{p}}{dt}=0}
$$
