# Newton's laws

## 1. Newton's law

**1. Newton's law** says, that if there isn't any force influencing the body, then it's staying still or moving at constant speed. Eg. we have a ball on a board, it's eventualy gonna stop because of friction (of the board and the air).

## 2. Newton's law

**2. Newton's law** says, that everyting, that is accelerating, there is a force involved. The equation is:

$$
\boxed{F=ma}
$$
