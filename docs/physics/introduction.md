# Physics

**[?]** Welcome to the Physics learning materials.

This guide is designed to help physics make practical sense, not just as a set of formulas you have to memorize. It will gradually walk you through the basics of understanding the physical world, getting comfortable with the core concepts, and tackling common problems you'll encounter when studying physics.

## List of all usefull equations:

### Kinematics:

$$
v=\frac{s}{t}
$$

$$
a=\frac{v}{t}
$$

$$
s=v_0t+\frac{1}{2}at^2
$$

**Circular movement:**

$$
f=\frac{1}{T}
$$

$$
T=\frac{1}{f}
$$

$$
\omega=2\pi f=\frac{2\pi}{T}
$$

$$
v=\omega r=2\pi rf=\frac{2\pi r}{T}
$$

$$
a=\frac{v^2}{r}=\omega^2r
$$

$$
F=m\frac{v^2}{r}=m\omega^2r
$$

### Dynamics:

$$
F=ma
$$

$$
F=mg
$$

$$
p=mv
$$

$$
p_1+p_2+...+p_n=const.\newline
\frac{dp}{dt}=0
$$

$$
F=F_0f=mgf\newline f\in(0;1)
$$

$$
F=\frac{\xi}{r}F_0=\frac{\xi}{r}mg
$$

### Energy:

$$
W=Fs
$$

$$
W=Fs\cos \theta
$$

$$
E_{grav. pot.}=mgh
$$

$$
E_{kin.}= \frac{1}{2}mv^2
$$
