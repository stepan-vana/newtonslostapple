# Friction

## Dry friction

is when there is a non-rollable body on a board, then there is a force, that is slowing down the body while it moves. Eg. a book sliding on a table, it will eventualy stop.  
Definition:

$$
\boxed{F=F_0f}
$$

where:

- $F$ is the friction
- $F_0$ is weight defined as $F=mg$
- $f$ is the **friction coefficient** and $0<f<1$ or $f\in (0;1)$

## Rolling resistance

is the same, but for things like balls (because it can roll).
Definition:

$$
\boxed{F=\frac{\xi}{r}F_0}
$$

where:

- $F$ is the friction
- $F_0$ is weight $F=mg$
- $\xi$ is the **rolling resistance coefficient**
- $r$ is the radius of the rolling object
