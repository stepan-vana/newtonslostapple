# What is a substance in chemistry?

**definition:** a substance is any pure given material that has a uniform composition of either elements or a compound with its given properties

*-example 1* : Teflon is a solid organic substance

*-example 2* : Nitrogen is a gaseous substance

*-example 3* : Flour is a mixture of organic compounds therefore its not a single substance

## Mixtures

**definition:** When two or more substances are mixed that can be physically separated *(note that this definition can be incorrect in specific scenearios)*

*-example 1* : a pile of thermite is a mixture of $Fe_2O_3$ and $Al$ powders

*-example 2* : ash in the air is a mixture of ash ($C_x$) and air (AKA smoke)

## Types of mixtures:

**1) heterogeneous** &rarr; generally defined as a mixture we can distinct individual components of the mixture rather easily such as: *sand in water*

**2) homogeneous** &rarr; a mixture of an uniform composition and phase such as: *$NaCl$ dissolved in water*

## Forms of mixtures:

**i) solutions** &rarr; A solution is a liquid homogenous mixture of a solvent and other chemical 

*-example:* $CO_2$ in $H_2O$ (sparkling water)

**ii) suspensions** &rarr; A suspension is a heterogeneous mixture of any given liquid and solid particles of a variable size that can be distinguished by the naked eye

*note: that suspensions will settle over time reflecting and strengthening the distinction more*

*-example:* vegetables in a broth

**iii) colloids** &rarr; A colloid is a mixture of insoluble (&rArr; solid) particles  that are so small and equally dispersed we cannot distinct them with the naked eye.
