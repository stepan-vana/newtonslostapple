# Chemistry

## the definition:

Chemistry is the study of a material and its change in time (for the most part)

## the history:

Chemistry as we know it today came to be during the time of the 17th and 18th century where it had separated from its more mystical and more religion and legend based alchemy in europe. Although it was alchemy before, it was still very important and people made quite the breakthroughs with it. For example the first fireworks were accidentally made in china from a mixture of honey, guano and sulfur which was heated and then spontaneously combusted.

## the basics:

Today chemistry relies on the periodic table, electron physics and thermodynamics
In chemistry we usually distinct 3 different states of matter:

Gas (G) a state where particles of a chemical freely roam at high speeds => they are compressible
Liquid (L) a state where particles of a chemical cannot roam completely freely but still have a lot of speed to them => they are not compressible
Solid (S) particles are locked with little to no speed to roam freely => they are not compressibley
